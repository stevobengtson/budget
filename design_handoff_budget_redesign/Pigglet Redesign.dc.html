The ramp flips, the meaning does not. Terracotta still means money going the wrong way and sage still means money you have — but tinted fills move from the 100–200 steps to 900, and the text on them from 700–800 to 400, keeping the same contrast relationship inverted.

Shadows stop carrying elevation. On the dark ground a shadow is nearly invisible, so cards separate by surface lightness instead: ground → surface → sunk. Only the screen itself keeps a shadow.

Muted text is neutral-400, not an opacity fade — the same rule as light mode, in the other direction.

Primary buttons keep a light label. The fill steps down to accent-500 and the label becomes the dark ground colour, because accent-400 text on accent-500 would not hold.

Implementation: every value above is a CSS custom property already in the sheet. Scope the overrides to :root[data-theme="dark"] and remap the semantic roles only — no component needs its own dark rules, and prefers-color-scheme can pick the default.

Turn 4 — transfers

A transfer needs a destination, so Transfer mode replaces Payee and Category with a From → To pair — and brings the category back when the destination is a card or loan, which is how the ledger already works.

4a

Transfer mode in the quick-add row

Picking Transfer swaps Payee for a From → To pair. Same row, same Enter, different fields.
Expense — for reference
Transfer — money moving between your own accounts
No payee and no category — nothing left your budget, it only moved ⇧⇥ swaps From and To
Transfer to a credit card or loan — a category appears

Because the destination is a card you owe on, you can tag the payment with a budget category. The money then shows as spent in that envelope without the arriving side counting as income.
